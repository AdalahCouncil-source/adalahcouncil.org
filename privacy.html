Privacy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Adalah Council, Inc. keeps the information it collects to a minimum. This page explains, in plain language, what is collected when you visit adalahcouncil.org, watch our videos, or donate, and what we do with it.

Site visits

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: how many people visit, which pages they read, how they found us, the general area they are in (city or region, not street address), and the kind of device and browser they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to collect this. It does not tell us your name or email address. We use these reports only to improve the site and our programs.

You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. To opt out, you can block cookies in your browser or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Fonts

The site's typefaces are served by Google Fonts. To show them, your browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records technical details of each visit, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Reading preferences

If you change the text size on a journal article, that choice is saved in your own browser so it stays the same on your next visit. It is not sent to us.

Videos

Videos on our Media page are hosted on YouTube and shown in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Preview images load from YouTube's image servers when the page opens. When you press play, the video loads from YouTube, and YouTube may set cookies and record that you watched it, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Donations

Donations are processed by Zeffy, a donation platform, on Zeffy's own pages. Zeffy shares the donor's name, contact details, and gift details with us so we can send receipts, thank donors, and keep the records a charity is required to keep. We never see or store your full card or bank details. Zeffy's handling of your information is covered by the privacy policy on its site.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address and message to reply and keep the correspondence only as long as it is needed.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not show advertising on this site.
  • The site has no accounts or logins and asks for no personal details to read it.

Children

The site is meant for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and the site will still work. You may ask us what information we hold about you (for example, donation records or emails) or ask us to correct or delete it, except for records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
